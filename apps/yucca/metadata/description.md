# Yucca

> Simple NVR video surveillance solution

Yucca is a simple NVR video surveillance solution for home and business. It is designed to support safe cities with thousands of cameras, subscriber services, and self-hosted home use.

[Android app](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.yucca.android&utm_source=umbrel)
