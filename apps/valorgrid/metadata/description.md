# ValorGrid

> Private and auditable investment portfolio tracking

ValorGrid is a local-first portfolio tracker for recording, importing and analysing investment movements with SQLite persistence, local backups and a browser UI. Portfolio data stays on your Runtipi server; market price lookups are sent only to the configured market data provider for the requested symbol.
