# Telegrapho

> Private copy and paste between your devices

Stop pasting sensitive text into third-party apps.

Telegrapho creates private realtime text channels between your devices using your self-hosted server.

Copy on mobile and receive instantly on desktop.

Your text. Your server. Your control.
