# Squeak Road

> Run your own anonymous market

Squeak Road lets you run an anonymous market where anyone can buy or sell anything.
As the admin of your market, you can curate which listings are allowed, and you can configure the fee rate that will be collected from sales.
Buyers can leave ratings for each completed sale, and the ratings are used to calculate a score for each seller account.
Warning: This app exposes your Lightning node pubkey. Be careful not to use any other app or invoice that links your Lightning node to your identity or your location.

## Default Credentials
- **Username**: `umbrel`
- **Password**: Generated automatically via the `APP_PASSWORD` field in your Runtipi app configuration.

## Inter-App Connections

This package integrates with **lightning** on the Runtipi shared network (`tipi_main_network`). You can customize the target hostnames, ports, and RPC credentials in the install form fields.
