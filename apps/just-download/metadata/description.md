# Just. Download.

> Download torrents. Just. Download.

Just Download allows you to easily download movies, books, games, etc. from your favourite indexers. It acts as a bridge between a Torznab feed (Jackett is recommended for making these) and transmission.

You just search, press download and you're done!

## Inter-App Connections

This package integrates with **transmission** on the Runtipi shared network (`tipi_main_network`). You can customize the target hostnames, ports, and RPC credentials in the install form fields.
